Welcome to Meckman Resume Builder

A free tool to help you build your first resume. Made for teens applying for their first job. Walk through the sections — contact info, school, anything you've done — and we'll lay it out as a clean, printable resume.

Privacy

Meckman Resume Builder does not require an account and does not store your resume, contact details, or any other information on our servers. All data is held in your browser's local storage on the device you're using. If you clear your browser data, use a different browser, or switch devices, the information cannot be recovered. To keep a permanent copy of your resume, use your browser's Print → Save as PDF option.

The "Help me word this" feature sends only the text you select to our AI provider (Cloudflare Workers AI) for a one-time rewrite. We do not log, retain, or use that text for any other purpose.

About the AI

The "Help me word this" feature uses AI, and AI can make mistakes. Always read what it gives you carefully before adding it to your resume. For something this important, ask a parent, teacher, mentor, school counselor, or a friend who's been through it to look over your finished resume with you. A second pair of eyes catches things you and the AI will miss.
